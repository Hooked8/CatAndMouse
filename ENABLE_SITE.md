# Make Pulseform live forever (free)

1. Open: https://github.com/Hooked8/CatAndMouse/settings/pages
2. Under **Build and deployment** → **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**
3. Branch: **gh-pages** / folder **/ (root)** → **Save**
4. After ~1 minute your site is: https://hooked8.github.io/CatAndMouse/
