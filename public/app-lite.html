How it opens

Line each dial’s amber tick with the amber witness ridge. Order rear→nose: 6 · 7 · 6 · 7. Pull the light grip to slide the vault open.

Inside

~22mm diameter × 42mm deep stash pocket for a key, USB stick, note, or ring.

Print

0.16–0.2mm layers, 3–4 walls, 25% gyroid. ~0.4mm print-in-place clearance. Gently free each dial after printing, then test the unlock.
